# Test Basis: PR-1530 — Make motor-scaffold more easily removable via click-helper

## 1. Overview
- **Ticket:** [PR-1530](https://pib-rocks.atlassian.net/browse/PR-1530)
- **Category:** Mechatronics
- **Assignee:** Jürgen Baier
- **Status:** Bereit (Ready for Development)

## 2. Objective
Redesign the motor scaffold mounting mechanism to enable quick, toolless, or single-click disengagement using an integrated click-helper latch/snap-fit mechanism.

## 3. Acceptance Criteria
1. **Click-Helper Latch Mechanism:** The motor scaffold incorporates a snap-fit/click-helper latch for easy, toolless or single-click disengagement.
2. **Structural Stability:** The scaffold remains securely locked and wobble-free during normal motor operation and joint movements.
3. **Removal Speed:** Disengagement takes less than 5 seconds with minimal manual effort.
4. **CAD Assets & Documentation:** Updated CAD models (.STEP / .3MF / .STL) and assembly documentation are published in the repository.

## 4. Verification Methods
- **Physical Fit & Mechanical Function Test:** Verify snap-fit locking force and click-helper release mechanism on physical 3D print assemblies.
- **Vibration & Load Stability Test:** Ensure no joint deflection or loosening occurs under peak motor torque.
- **Assembly/Disassembly Time Metric:** Confirm removal time is under 5 seconds without requiring screwdrivers or specialized tools.
